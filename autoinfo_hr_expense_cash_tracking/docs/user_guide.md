# User Guide

## Main Flow

1. Employee creates expense lines and fills in an analytic account on every line.
2. Employee groups lines into an expense sheet and requests validation.
3. Reviewers process the sheet through manager, accounting, and final approval tiers.
4. If a reviewer returns the sheet, the document goes back to draft with a reason in chatter and on the form.
5. Employee corrects the sheet and requests validation again.
6. Finance marks the reimbursement as completed after cash is paid.

## Roles

- Employee: creates and submits expense sheets
- Reviewer chain: validates the sheet through tier validation
- Reimbursement manager: marks the cash reimbursement as completed
- Accounting and executive groups: can review expense sheets according to visibility rules

## Exports

- Detail XLSX: exports line-level information for a selected expense sheet
- Summary XLSX: exports sheet-level information for a date range through the wizard
